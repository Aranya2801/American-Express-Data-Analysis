Strategic Insights

💳
Premium Strategy

70% of new 2025 card acquisitions are fee-based premium products, up from 62% in 2022. Card fees now represent 10.5% of total revenue.

🌍
Network Expansion

Merchant acceptance grew to 160M locations worldwide in 2025 — a 5× increase since 2017, closing the gap with Visa and Mastercard.

🧑‍💼
Gen Z Dominance

Millennials and Gen Z now represent 65% of new consumer card acquisitions globally, ensuring long-term franchise health.

📉
Credit Quality

Write-off rate held at 1.9% in 2025 — well below industry average. Amex's premium customer base drives best-in-class credit performance.
